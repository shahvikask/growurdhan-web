Home · About

A private place for the household’s records.

GrowUrDhan helps Indian families organise their financial records in one place — while keeping individual ownership visible and the data on the device. It does not open a brokerage account and it does not ask for a GrowUrDhan login.

What it is

A local-first tracker. You add family members, import statements on the device, and see each person’s holdings next to a household total. Ownership stays on the member.

What it is not

Not a broker, bank, or SEBI-registered adviser. It does not place trades, hold money, or tell you what to buy. The app is not on Google Play yet, and this website does not offer an APK.

The names on the screens

Aarav, Nisha, Inaaya, and Kapoor HUF are a made-up household. The rupee figures are illustrative. They are not customers, reviews, or a live portfolio.

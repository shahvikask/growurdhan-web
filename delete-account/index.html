Delete account

Play listing deletion instructions. Last updated 22 September 2026.

GrowUrDhan does not create a cloud account. There is no user-id on our servers to erase. Deletion is local, plus any encrypted backup folder you chose.

1. Delete the vault on the phone

  1. Open GrowUrDhan and use in-app delete / reset if the build offers it.
  2. Or: Android Settings → Apps → GrowUrDhan → Storage → Clear data, then Uninstall.

That removes the on-device encrypted vault, CAS files the app retained, and licence cache on that device.

2. Delete backups you made

If you scheduled an encrypted backup to Google Drive or OneDrive, delete those files in the folder you picked. GrowUrDhan cannot see or empty that folder for you.

3. Play purchases

The ₹249 lifetime licence and ₹299 annual support are Google Play products. Cancel support in Google Play subscriptions. Refunds, if any, follow Google Play policy. Uninstalling does not by itself cancel a Play subscription.

4. This website

growurdhan.com stores no account. If you wrote to us by email, ask that thread to be deleted; we do not run a marketing list.

What we cannot delete

We cannot remotely wipe a phone we do not control, a CAS PDF that still sits in your Downloads, or a Play receipt in your Google account.

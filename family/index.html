Home · Family

The household, without mixing who owns what.

Family is the point of GrowUrDhan. A spouse’s NPS is not filed under your PAN. An HUF demat is not a personal folio. The screen can still show one household.

Who is included

All four match the app. Untick someone to see their card amount come out — that partial sum is not household net worth.

What this page is not

Not tax advice, not a reason to form an HUF, and not a merged bank account. It is a way to look at records you already keep.

Questions before you install

Is GrowUrDhan a brokerage?

No. It does not open a demat, take orders or hold funds.

Can it buy or sell investments?

No. It is a records tool.

Does it store my books on a server?

No mandatory cloud database. Statements are parsed on the phone. Backup is an encrypted file in a folder you pick.

Do I need a phone number?

No.

Can I track spouse, children and HUF?

Yes. Each is a member with their own PAN.

Can I import CAS?

Yes. CDSL, NSDL, CAMS, KFin and NPS on the device.

What if I lose the phone?

Restore from a backup you hold, with the same Play account as the ₹249 licence.

Is this investment advice?

No.

Does it connect to my bank?

No.

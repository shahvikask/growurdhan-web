Questions before you install

Short answers. The security and pricing pages are the longer versions.

Is GrowUrDhan a brokerage?

No. It does not open a demat account, take orders, or hold funds.

Does it give investment advice?

No. It is a records tool. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell.

Does GrowUrDhan store my books on its servers?

There is no GrowUrDhan cloud database. Statements are parsed on the phone. An optional encrypted backup goes only to a folder you pick. GrowUrDhan does not keep a copy.

Do I need a phone number?

No. There is no OTP login and no GrowUrDhan cloud account.

What can I record?

Mutual funds, stocks, NPS, PPF, fixed deposits, gold, and other assets you add. CAS sources named on the product are NSDL, CDSL, CAMS, KFin, and NPS. Files are read on the device.

Can I keep self, spouse, children, and HUF separate?

Yes. Each member keeps their own records. The home screen can show a household total without mixing who owns what. Family members are unlimited.

What does ₹249 lifetime mean?

After the trial, a one-time Play purchase (gud_lifetime) on a genuine install, tied to that Google Play account. It is not a promise that the company, the Play listing, or every parser will exist forever. See Pricing.

What is the trial?

30 days on a genuine Play install. The clock is device-bound. It does not travel when you change phones.

What is the ₹299 support year?

Optional, and only after the lifetime licence. If that year lapses, the vault still opens.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Without an encrypted backup you made, the books on that phone are gone. Restore uses the backup file you hold and the same Play account as the licence.

Does it connect to my bank or broker?

No demat password, bank login, or OTP. It does not connect to your bank.

Is the app on Google Play?

Not yet. There is no APK on this website. The package name planned for the listing is in.growurdhan.app.

Are the Kapoor names and rupee figures real?

No. Aarav, Nisha, Inaaya, and Kapoor HUF are an illustrative household used on the screens. They are not customers.

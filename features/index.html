Home · Features

See it, organise it, understand it, keep it.

GrowUrDhan is a records app for an Indian household. It is not a broker and it does not fetch statements for you.

See

Home

Household net worth, with assets and loans beside it, plus allocation and recent activity.

Family

The flagship view. Each member has a card. The house total does not erase the owner.

Holdings and accounts

Cost, value, and where a folio or demat sits — including a loan account.

Organise

People

Self, spouse, children, and an HUF. Unlimited members. Roles stay labeled.

Statements you already have

You pick a CDSL, NSDL, CAMS, KFin, or NPS file. There is no scheduled download from a depository.

Documents

Imported files stay listed on the phone: CAS, NPS, a sale deed, an HUF PAN — whatever you chose to keep.

Understand

Net worth

Assets minus loans. On the illustrative home screen that is ₹2.61 Cr, not the ₹3.07 Cr asset total.

Mix and goals

Allocation by type, and goals you track yourself. Not a recommendation to buy or sell.

Manual rows

PPF, deposits, gold, and other assets that never appear on a CAS can be entered by hand.

Protect

On the device

No GrowUrDhan cloud database. The vault is encrypted. Keys stay in Android Keystore.

No broker login

No demat password, bank OTP, or GrowUrDhan account.

Backup you hold

Optional encrypted file in a folder you pick. If you skip it, a lost phone means lost books.

Names and rupees on the public screens are the illustrative Kapoor household, not a customer.

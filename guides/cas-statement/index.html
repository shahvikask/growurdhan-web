A CAS file is a record. It is not a login.

A consolidated account statement is the file depositories and registrars already send you — commonly CDSL or NSDL for demat, and CAMS or KFin for mutual fund folios. NPS has its own statement. This page explains the idea. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Why people download one

Holdings sit in more than one place. A monthly statement is a way to see cost and units without giving an app your broker password.

What GrowUrDhan does with it

You pick the file on the phone. Parsing happens on the device. There is no GrowUrDhan server that receives the PDF. See Security.

What it does not do

It does not fetch the statement from your email for you, connect to your broker, or place an order.

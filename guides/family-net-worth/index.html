Net worth is what remains after loans.

In this product, household net worth means recorded assets minus recorded loans. A list of funds and deposits alone is an asset total. GrowUrDhan does not call that net worth when a loan is on the books.

The illustrative house

On the sample home screen, assets are ₹2.24 Cr, loans are ₹38.0 L, and net worth is ₹1.86 Cr. The family screen repeats that net worth and still shows each member. Those names are a sample household. They are not a customer, not your records, and not a forecast.

Why the split matters

A spouse’s NPS is not the same owner as an HUF demat or a child’s Sukanya record. Adding the figures is useful. Mixing the labels is not. Member cards on the family screen are those separate amounts. They are not a second definition of net worth.

What this number is not

Not a legal valuation, not a tax assessment, and not advice to borrow or repay. It is only as complete as the assets and loans you recorded.

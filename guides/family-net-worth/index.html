Home · Guide

One number for the house, owners kept apart.

Family net worth, in this product, means the combined value of assets you have recorded for the people in the vault. It is a household view. It is not a legal valuation, a tax return, or advice.

Why the split matters

A spouse’s NPS is not the same owner as an HUF demat account or a child’s Sukanya record. Adding the figures is useful. Mixing the labels is not.

How the app shows it

Each member keeps their own rows. The home screen can show the house. The illustrative Kapoor figures on the homepage are a demo, not a sample customer.

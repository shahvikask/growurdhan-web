Tracking a family’s wealth is not the same as managing it.

“Family wealth management” often means a firm that advises and executes. GrowUrDhan does neither. It is a private tracker: you organise what you already hold, on your phone.

Who it is for

A household whose mutual funds, shares, NPS, PPF, deposits, or gold are spread across more than one person and more than one statement.

Who it is not for

Anyone looking for a broker, a tip, or a guaranteed return. Those are not on offer.

Folios, next to everything else.

A mutual fund portfolio is often only one slice of a household: spouse NPS, PPF, a child’s Sukanya, an HUF demat. GrowUrDhan is for seeing those together, with the owner still visible.

CAMS and KFin CAS files are parsed on the device, the same way as other statements. The app does not recommend funds and does not start a SIP.

The statements are scattered. The owners should not be.

A household’s mutual funds, shares, NPS, PPF, deposits, gold, and a home loan rarely live in one login. GrowUrDhan is the place those records sit together, with the name of the owner still on the row.

What you can see, organise, and protect

You bring the statement. The phone reads it.

There is no monthly download running in the background. When you have a CAS or NPS file, you pick it. CDSL, NSDL, CAMS, KFin, and NPS are the statement types the product is built to read on the device.

Security · Privacy

A backup is a file you hold

If the phone is lost and you never wrote an encrypted backup, those books are gone. Restore uses the file you kept and the same Google Play account as the ₹249 licence. GrowUrDhan does not keep a copy. The 30-day trial clock stays on the first device.

Before you install

Is this a brokerage account?

No. GrowUrDhan does not open a demat, take orders, or hold funds.

Will it download my CAS every month?

No. You choose a file you already have. Parsing stays on the phone.

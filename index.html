Security page

If you change phones

The books on a lost phone are gone unless you kept an optional encrypted backup in a folder you control. Restore uses that file and the same Google Play account as the ₹249 licence. The 30-day trial clock stays on the first device. GrowUrDhan does not keep a copy of the backup.

Questions before you install

Is GrowUrDhan a brokerage?

No. It does not open a demat, take orders or hold funds.

Does it store my books on a server?

No mandatory cloud database. Statements are parsed on the phone.

Do I need a phone number?

No OTP login and no GrowUrDhan cloud account.

Privacy

Last updated 25 September 2026.

GrowUrDhan runs on your Android phone. Financial data should not leave the device.

This website does not collect email or phone numbers. There is no mandatory cloud database, no server-side CAS processing, and no mobile number login.

What may go online: Play updates; optional NAV requests you start; Google Play Billing; an optional encrypted backup to a folder you pick.

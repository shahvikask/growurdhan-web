Policy

Privacy

This is the Play listing privacy text for GrowUrDhan. Last updated 22 September 2026.

GrowUrDhan is a private family wealth tracker that runs on your Android phone. The product rule is simple: financial data should not leave the device. A privacy failure is treated as a release-blocking defect.

Who we are

GrowUrDhan publishes the Android app in.growurdhan.app. This website does not collect email, phone numbers, or waitlist data.

Default boundary

  • No mandatory cloud database or portfolio API.
  • No server-side CAS processing.
  • No automatic upload of statement PDFs.
  • No third-party analytics that receives holdings.
  • No raw PAN in ordinary logs, URLs, or error reports.
  • No mobile number and no OTP login.

What the app stores on the device

Family members, holdings, CAS-derived records, documents you file in the Record Book, licence state, and an encrypted vault. Encryption keys are held with the Android Keystore. GrowUrDhan cannot read the books.

What may go online

  • Application updates from Google Play.
  • Optional market / reference data you request (close prices, NAV). Those requests must not carry your portfolio.
  • Google Play Billing for gud_lifetime and gud_support_annual. Google processes the card. GrowUrDhan never sees it.
  • Optional encrypted backup to a Drive or OneDrive folder you pick. You hold the folder and the key. This is opt-in.

What we do not collect

We do not create a GrowUrDhan cloud account. We do not ask for a phone number. We do not run a newsletter from this site. We do not sell holdings data.

Your rights on-device

  • View and export your records without an internet connection.
  • Lock the vault and mask amounts on screen.
  • Delete the local store the same way you delete any other local app data. See Delete account.

Children

The app is for users 18 years or older. You may record a child’s holdings as a family member you control. The child is not the account holder.

This website

growurdhan.com is a static site. It sets no marketing cookies and hosts no form that stores your email on our servers.

Changes

If the default boundary changes (for example a new optional backup), this page will say so before the Play listing asks you to accept it. Do not expect a second, different privacy policy on another host.

Privacy

This page describes the product rule as published on the site. It is not a certification.

This website

growurdhan.com is a static site. It does not ask for your email, phone number, PAN, or statements. There is no signup form.

The app

Family members, holdings, and statement-derived records are stored on the phone. There is no GrowUrDhan cloud database and no server-side parsing of CAS files. The vault is encrypted on the device. Keys stay with Android Keystore. GrowUrDhan cannot read the books.

What can leave the phone

  • Google Play for install, updates, and billing. GrowUrDhan does not see the card.
  • An optional encrypted backup, only to a folder you choose. GrowUrDhan does not keep a copy.

The app does not ask for a demat password, bank login, or OTP, and it does not connect to your bank.

Deletion

There is no cloud user account to erase. Clear the app’s data, uninstall, and delete backup files you created. See Delete account.

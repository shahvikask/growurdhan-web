A statement you choose, read on the phone.

GrowUrDhan does not log into your depository, registrar, broker, or bank, and it does not download a CAS for you. If you hand it a file, this is where that file goes.

Local storage

There is no GrowUrDhan cloud database and no server-side parsing of statements.

Encryption

The vault is encrypted on the device. Keys stay with Android Keystore. That is not a claim that no risk exists. It is where the keys are.

Backup

Optional. The encrypted file goes to a folder you pick. GrowUrDhan does not keep a copy. Without that file, a lost phone means lost books.

What never leaves for us

No demat password, bank login, or OTP. No phone-number account on this product.

Your financial data stays with you.

If you give GrowUrDhan a CAS statement, this page is the answer to where that information goes.

Local data storage

Family members, holdings, CAS records, documents, licence state and the vault live on the phone. No GrowUrDhan cloud database. No server-side CAS processing.

Encryption at rest

The vault is encrypted on the device. Keys are held with the Android Keystore. GrowUrDhan cannot read the books.

Backup encryption

Optional encrypted backup to a folder you pick. You hold the folder and the key. Opt-in.

Password, PIN and biometrics

The vault can be locked on the phone. Amounts can be masked. Device lock follows Android.

Statement processing

Files are parsed on the device. There is no automatic upload of statement PDFs.

Network communication

Play updates; optional NAV requests you start (must not carry the portfolio); Play Billing; optional backup to a folder you pick.

No brokerage credentials

No demat password, bank login or OTP. The app does not connect to your bank and does not trade.

Data deletion

There is no cloud user-id. Clear app data, uninstall, and delete backup files you made. See Delete account.

Analytics and SDKs

No third-party analytics that receives holdings. No raw PAN in ordinary logs. Play Billing is Google’s checkout.

Restore and device change

Restore uses the backup you hold and the same Play account as the ₹249 licence. The trial clock is device-bound.

Terms

GrowUrDhan is a personal wealth organisation application for users 18 or older. It records investments and other assets you choose to enter or import. It does not execute trades, hold customer funds, or provide personalised investment advice. It is not a SEBI-registered adviser, broker, or bank.

The Android app is not publicly listed yet. When it is, checkout is Google Play: a 30-day trial on a genuine install, then a ₹249 lifetime licence (gud_lifetime), with an optional ₹299 support year. “Lifetime” is described on the pricing page. It is not a promise that the company or every parser will exist forever.

Screens on this website use an illustrative household. Those names and amounts are not customers or results.
